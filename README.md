## About me

I'm a computer science student specializing in **Artificial Intelligence**, based in **Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo**. I am interested in the full lifecycle of technology products: understanding a real problem, designing a reliable architecture, building the application, and progressively integrating intelligent capabilities.

My current development experience is primarily oriented toward **Python, backend development, web applications, APIs, and database-driven systems**. I also work with frontend technologies to build complete user experiences.

I don't want to use AI as a black box. My long-term goal is to understand the mathematics, algorithms, architectures, and engineering constraints behind intelligent systems—and learn how to deploy them reliably in real-world environments.

- 🎓 **Currently studying:** Artificial Intelligence — undergraduate level (BAC 3).
